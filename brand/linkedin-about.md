## Overview

BABAHALA Group is an independent brand strategy and consumer insights firm. We tell you what your customers actually want, and what to do about it, in four working days.

You get a strategy presentation built to survive the room it is shown in. The recommendation sits inside an organizing framework. Your stakeholders can see where the business stands, the idea that moves it forward, and how every action connects to the outcome in your brief. Underneath it sits the evidence. Every insight traces to interview turns you can read, and every market claim to a source with the date it was retrieved.

The speed comes from the panel. Respondents are synthetic, and everything they are built from is real. Each one starts from an anonymized US Census household record. They are drawn in proportion to how common people like them are among the buyers you screen for, not among the general population. Their attitudes come from a scan of current public evidence, run fresh for every study. A moderator then interviews each of them for ninety minutes and probes past the first answer, because the first answer a person gives is usually a rationalization.

Every study is audited before it reaches you, against the handful of places research goes soft, and the results go in the deliverable. Findings are directional and never presented as projectable. We say so on the page, where your stakeholders can read it.

The work is directed start to finish by a principal strategist with twenty years in consumer insights and brand strategy. That experience runs across spirits and beer, food and beverage, retail, technology and financial services. No study is handed to a junior team.

For insights and brand teams who need an answer inside the planning cycle, and for owner-led businesses for whom research at this depth has been out of reach.
